Understand These Results: Personality Profile

This guide explains how to interpret the internal personality profile in practical hiring language.

Use for fit and coaching, not intelligence ranking

1) What This Profile Measures

The profile estimates behavioral tendencies in how a candidate communicates, decides, executes, collaborates, and creates momentum.

  • How they process information and make decisions.
  • How they influence others and respond to pressure.
  • What kinds of work naturally energize them.
  • Where they may need structure, support, or role alignment.

2) Reading The Output

Output AreaHow To Use It
Primary Profile TypeFast summary of likely operating style in real work settings.
Top Behavioral TendenciesSignals where the candidate may be naturally strong (communication, structure, pace, etc.).
Execution / Influence / Collaboration PatternsUse to match the person to role demands and team dynamics.
Risk FlagsAreas to probe in interview before deciding (speed, consistency, adaptability, ownership).

3) How To Use In Hiring

  1. Start with role requirements (pace, autonomy, team leadership, systems complexity).
  2. Compare profile tendencies against those requirements.
  3. Use interview questions to confirm or challenge the profile signals.
  4. Document evidence before making final decisions.

Do not use personality profile as a stand-alone pass/fail gate.

4) Suggested Interview Prompts

  • "Tell me about a time you inherited a broken process. What did you change first?"
  • "How do you keep a team aligned when priorities shift quickly?"
  • "Describe a decision where you had incomplete information. How did you decide?"
  • "What type of work gives you energy, and what type drains you?"

5) Coaching Plan After Hire

  • Leverage strengths: assign early wins in the candidate's high-energy areas.
  • Protect weak spots: add checklists, SOPs, cadence reviews, or paired ownership.
  • Track adaptation: revisit fit after 30/60/90 days with manager notes.

Best practice: combine profile + cognitive results + structured interview + reference checks.
